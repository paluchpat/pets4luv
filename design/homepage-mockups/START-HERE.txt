PETS4LUV — FOUR HOMEPAGE DESIGN DIRECTIONS
Prepared November 5, 2026

These are static visual concepts for discussion, not implemented web pages.
Animal photos are AI-generated and animal details/copy are illustrative, not current adoption listings.
Each concept includes adoption discovery, weekly highlights, and a conversational helper.

01 — WARM WELCOME
Cream, forest green, soft shapes, and friendly serif typography.
Focus: warmth, reassurance, and approachable adoption.

02 — BIG PERSONALITIES
Yellow, cobalt, bold rounded lettering, and playful animal portraits.
Focus: joy, personality, and a memorable visual identity.

03 — FIND YOUR MATCH
White and teal, structured filters, immediate animal results, and prominent chat.
Focus: useful search and helping visitors discover suitable animals quickly.

04 — EVERY ANIMAL, A STORY
Large photography, burgundy, editorial typography, and featured animal stories.
Focus: emotional connection and the rescue's mission.

REVIEW PROMPT
Ask each reviewer to choose a first and second favorite, then name the elements they would keep or change. Layout, typography, colors, and chat placement can be combined across directions.

NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
Use pets4luv-logo-original.png as the actual logo asset. Generated concept images may approximate small logo lettering.
Search controls, donation links, and chat are visual treatments only; they do not change the implementation scope or activate services. The search-first mockup illustrates draft filter selections before pressing Show matches; final behavior must clearly distinguish applied filters.
Use real approved animal photos and verified information before launch. Responsive/mobile layouts will follow selection of a direction.
